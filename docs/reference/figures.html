The figures of Tre

Every person and animal of the village, built on a grid of quarter blocks (src/world/fine.js). Each row: at rest and mid-step, from the front and at three-quarter; in the wind (at rest, with the breeze at full strength blowing to the left, into the faces: the hair, the cloth, and the tails move, src/world/sway.js); the last column is the far level (the coarse figure that the game draws for a figure far from the hero: 30 blocks at the near zoom, 45 at the far zoom). The people are at their true size; the animals are drawn larger.

The last sheet is a record of a comparison: the same bodies, with the head and the hair as smooth low balls (an icosphere of level 1 in the flat tones, with the ink at the outline) in place of the stepped blocks. The owner chose the stepped heads; the game draws them.

The figures of Tre

Every figure of the game as a piece of a board game: on a round base of quarter blocks, on a square of dó paper. Tap a piece to lift it up close: drag to turn it and to tilt the view, pinch or use the wheel to zoom, and tap two times to go back to the first view. The switches show the walk, the wind, the moods of the face, the night, and the far level (the figure that the game draws far from the hero). The address keeps what is on the screen, so that a link opens the same piece.

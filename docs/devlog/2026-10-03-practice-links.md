# A link to one activity, and practice that is never the same

*3 October 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-10-03-practice-links.vi.md)*

A parent wanted to send a child straight to one thing, for example the multiplication table, with no need to play the story from the start. A link is easy to make. Two questions are not: what may the link hold, and how can a child do the same practice many times and not get bored?

## What we found

- **A link goes anywhere.** It stays in the history of a browser and of a chat, and anyone who gets it can open it.
- **Mixed practice beats practice in blocks.** In 54 classes of grade 7, mixed practice gave 61% on a surprise test one month later, against 38% for practice in blocks (d = 0.83) ([Rohrer et al. 2020](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1237752)).
- **The best gap between reviews grows** with the time that a person must remember ([Cepeda et al. 2006](https://www.yorku.ca/ncepeda/publications/CPVWR2006.html)).
- **The wish to play again comes from a feeling of skill and of choice,** not from rewards or graphics ([Ryan, Rigby & Przybylski 2006](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf)).

## What we decided

- **The link holds only the name of the activity** (`?practice=cay-lua`), never a name, a profile, or any data of a child. It opens the child's own profile on that device, or makes one in a few taps, with no prologue.
- **What the child learns is saved.** The place of the hero in the story does not change, unless the child chooses to stay.
- **Each activity has a story file,** so that we can watch it before a child plays it.
- **The facts come back by a schedule, not by chance.** Each fact of the table has its own memory, shared by all the activities of multiplication. A fact that the child misses comes back later in the same set, in another form; a fact that the child knows comes back after days. Two tasks of the same table never come one after another.
- **Around the fact, everything changes:** the form of the task, the place, the time of day, the weather, and one small event. The child's earlier work stays in the world: the planted paddies grow on the next days.
- **The child chooses** from two or three tasks, all at a good level. A bigger task is on offer for pride, and it is never needed.
- **The parent page lists the activities,** with a button to copy or share each link.

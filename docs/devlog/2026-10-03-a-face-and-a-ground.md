# A face that reads, and a ground that is not a table

*3 October 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-10-03-a-face-and-a-ground.vi.md)*

On 3 October we looked at the game on a phone, and two things were wrong. The face of a figure was a thin band between the hair and a stack of steps under the chin, and the steps looked like a neck with rings. The six hair styles looked almost the same from the front, and a child of the village had the face of a grown-up. The ground was a flat field of one color, so the land looked like a board.

## What we found

- **The face of a small child has a known shape.** The "baby schema" is a set of features: a large head, a round face, and big eyes. Adults see faces with more of these features as more cute, and they want more to care for them ([Glocker et al. 2009](https://www.researchwithrutgers.org/en/publications/baby-schema-in-infant-faces-induces-cuteness-perception-and-motiv/)).
- **Words were not enough to decide.** We made a few versions on a copy of the game, drawn by the game's own code, and the owner of the project chose from the pictures ([the faces](https://github.com/sntran/Tre/blob/main/docs/media/issue-19/faces.jpg); [the ground](https://github.com/sntran/Tre/blob/main/docs/media/issue-20/ground.jpg)).
- **A texture alone did much.** A pattern in the shader of the ground, with no new shapes, made the village look much less flat.

## What we decided

- **A child looks like a child:** the eyes low under a large forehead, big and dark; thin brows; a small mouth close to the eyes; a short round lower face; rosy cheeks; and no nose. A grown-up has the eyes at the middle of the head, a jaw, and a nose. The neck is one short band in shadow.
- **Each hair style differs from the front:** a fringe, a topknot with a red tie, long hair with a part, two braids, a low bun, the tuft of a small child.
- **The ground is a print:** grass in two greens in soft patches with short strokes; earth roads with grain, small stones, and the ruts of wheels; sand with ripples. The pattern is in the shader and costs no shapes.
- **The land has a shape:** roads through the paddies run on low banks, roads on dry land lie a little lower than the grass, and the low land has mounds, ditches, ponds, and tufts of grass.
- **We decide a look from pictures made with the code of the game,** not from descriptions.

# A count, not a clock

*7 October 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-10-07-a-count-not-a-clock.vi.md)*

The slingshot of a raid teaches the number line: the child sees how far the enemy is, chooses a pull, and sees the stone land short or long. So that a child who only uses the big button can play too, the button pulled the band while it was down: one step at once, then four steps each second. We played the raids as a child of six with the button, and we lost every raid. Then the parent area said that the child was bored.

## What we found

- **A child cannot stop at a step that comes faster than the child can react.** A step is a short step on the road (half a block of the world of the game), so the fourth post is 20 steps, a hold of about five seconds, and a new step comes each 250 ms. In one study, the mean simple reaction time of children of six was 467 ms, and of children of five 580 ms ([Kiselev, Espy, and Sheffield 2009, table 1](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1043&context=dcnlfacpub)). A child who lets go at the right time is late by one or two steps, and the enemy walks on.
- **The button measured time, but the game logged a count.** Each miss went into the learning log as a mistake of counting. After the raids, the parent area said "Đếm đến 120: 17/43", and the weekly note said that the child "nhiều lần làm nhanh mà sai xa. Có thể Mai đang chán ở đó". The child was not bored, and the child could count.
- **A raid that nobody can win stops the story.** The iron for the horse of Gióng needs two won raids. The raids come back each morning with the same strength, so a child who loses can never go on.

## What we decided

- **A count, not a clock.** The child sets each count of a raid with presses or with the ticks of a drag, and sees the count before it acts. With the button, one press is one post. No count grows with the time that a button is held.
- **The count shows on the road.** A small ring lies at the count while the child chooses it. The child still estimates: the choice is the count, and the ring shows the count, not the answer.
- **The story never stops at a raid.** The first raids of each tool, and the raids after a loss, are smaller and slower, and after two losses a helper stands at the wall.
- **The note gives only reasons that the game can know.** When a mistake can come from the controls, the note says what happened, not why.

## The limit of what we know

The study measured a simple reaction to a light, not a release on a moving target, which is harder. We did not test the new pull with children yet. The details are in issue #55.

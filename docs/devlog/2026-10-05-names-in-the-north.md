# Ông Dương and chị Hến: names in the north

*5 October 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-10-05-names-in-the-north.vi.md)*

Earlier today we gave the people of Xóm Ruộng names by the order of birth: Cô Năm, Chị Ba, Chú Tư, Ông Cả (see [Cô Năm, not cô Cấy](2026-10-05-names-words-and-corn.md)). The owner of the project grew up in the south, where everyone is called that way. He asked for the order of birth because the people of the game had no names, and he asked us to find a better way for the north if there is one. Is the order of birth the way of a northern village?

## What we found

- **The order of birth is used in the north too,** with Cả for the first child: "anh Cả, chị Ba…" in the northern countryside ([Mien Cao 2018](https://dothogiadinh.vn/cach-xung-ho-ten-cua-nguoi-viet.html)).
- **But the north also names people by their children.** "In the countryside of the north, a father and a mother usually call each other by the name or the small name of a child: bố thằng A, mẹ cái B, bu con Cún." In many places of Thanh Hóa, old people are called by the name of the first child, with no order: "ông Dương, bà Dương" are the father and the mother of anh Dương (same source). A northern couple was "ông Trung bà Trung" for years, after their first son ([Cao Thu Cúc](https://caothucuc.wordpress.com/2014/06/16/cach-xung-ho-cua-nguoi-viet-nam/)). In the south, the name of the first child goes with the order: "chị Hai Tùng, anh Ba Hiển" (Mien Cao).
- **A village does not say the own names of old people.** "Con cháu kiêng (cử) tên, không nói đến tên ông bà cha mẹ", and many villages keep this custom (Toan Ánh, *Nếp cũ*, in [Znews](https://znews.vn/tuc-kieng-goi-ten-to-tien-cua-nguoi-viet-post1648087.html)).
- **Village children have a small, plain name** (tên tục), so that they are "easy to raise": Cu, Tí, Tèo, Cún, Cò, Hến, Sún ([Soha](https://soha.vn/vi-sao-nguoi-xua-hay-dat-ten-xau-cho-tre-198260905160854986.htm)).
- **The limit of what we know:** these sources are popular writings, not studies. Nobody wrote down how villagers spoke in the time of the Hùng Kings, and the game speaks today's Vietnamese in all eras.

## What we decided

- **In the north,** a grown-up keeps the order of birth with Cả (cô Năm, chú Tư); an old person is called by the name of the first child (the old drummer is **ông Dương**); and a child or a young person by the own small name (the duck girl is **chị Hến**).
- **In the south,** everyone keeps the order of birth from Hai (cô Sáu, chú Năm, ông Hai, chị Tư). When two people of one place would have the same name, the name of the first child comes after the order, as in "chị Hai Tùng".
- **A title of the work is a real name too,** so the people of Phù Đổng stay bác thợ rèn and bà lang.
- **In English,** a name gets a short meaning the first time: "Ông Dương: an old man is called by the name of his first child, Dương." So a child who grows up far from Vietnam learns the custom, not only the name.

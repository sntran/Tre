# History is told by somebody

*29 September 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-09-29-history-is-told-by-somebody.vi.md)*

A game of history must choose whose story it tells. The history of Vietnam has wars with its neighbors, peoples who lived in the center and the south before the Việt came, and the wars of the 20th century, which families still remember in different and painful ways. Children of many families play Tre, Chinese-American families among them.

## What we found

- **Young children can compare two tellings.** Children in the first grades can compare two tellings of a story and say why they differ ([NRC 2005](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/10126/chapter/4)).
- **The weighing of written sources comes later.** It comes in the upper grades, with much help from a teacher ([Barton 1997](http://thenhier.ca/en/node/312.html); [VanSledright 2002](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/000283120390041089)).
- **A story that the child only watches does not teach.** Interesting details that the task does not use lower what children remember ([Sundararajan & Adesope 2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-020-09522-4)).

## What we decided

- **Two kinds of text.** The narrator tells only facts that have a primary source. The people of the world tell what they saw, remember, and believe. Their words are theirs, and the narrator never takes a side.
- **Many voices.** People of other origins live where history put them: Minh Hương families in Hội An, Hoa families in Chợ Lớn, Cham villages in the center, Khmer villages in the Mekong delta, traders from Japan, China, and Europe in the ports. They are neighbors, not visitors. Two people can see the same event in different ways, and each view is the most honest version of how a real person on that side would see it.
- **Enemies are not evil.** The soldiers of an enemy army are people who follow orders. They retreat or surrender, and some of them wanted to go home. The game shows no blood and no deaths. It speaks of the rulers and the armies of that time, never of peoples today, and it uses no insults or old words of hate.
- **Each story has a mark:** "Legend" or "History".
- **The country grows with history.** In the time of the Hùng Kings the map shows only the north. The land grows south chapter by chapter, and the map shows Champa and the Khmer with respect, as neighbors with their own history.
- **The story stops at the 19th century.** The colonial time and the wars of the 20th century come into the game only through service projects of today: bridges for children in the mountains, homes after a typhoon, clean water for far villages.
- **A person who knows the history of Vietnam checks each note** before a release. This check has not happened yet. It must happen before a release.

From this rule came a second one later: when nobody knows, the game says so (see [Where is Núi Trâu?](2026-10-03-where-is-nui-trau.md)).

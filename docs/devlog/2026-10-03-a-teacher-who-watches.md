# A teacher who watches, and learning to learn inside the work

*3 October 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-10-03-a-teacher-who-watches.vi.md)*

A person in a game often gives a task, says a line, and waits. A real teacher does not. When you give a child a task, you watch, you see what went wrong, and you change the help or the task. We wanted the people of Tre to do the same.

## What we found

- **The oldest finding about tutors is to watch and adjust.** A good tutor gets the child interested, makes the task small enough, keeps the child on the goal, marks what matters, controls frustration, and shows how ([Wood, Bruner & Ross 1976](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7610.1976.tb00381.x)).
- **It works in software too.** Human tutoring had an effect of d = 0.79, and tutoring systems that react to each step had d = 0.76, nearly the same ([VanLehn 2011](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ946764)).

Then we asked a second question. The idea was that a child must first learn how to learn: to look, to know what was right or wrong, and to adjust. Should "learning to learn" be the first skill of Tre, taught before the math? We first wrote it in that way. Then we checked the evidence, and it said something different:

- **Learning to learn can be taught, and it pays most in math** (an average effect of 0.69 across 84 studies; [Dignath & Büttner 2008](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11409-008-9029-x)). In young children it worked better when it came from teachers or the task materials ([Eberhart, Ingendahl & Bryce 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11409-024-09405-x)).
- **But not as a separate, general skill.** General skills taught apart from content do not work ([Tricot & Sweller 2014](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-013-9243-1)), and brain training does not carry over to other things ([Sala et al. 2019](https://online.ucpress.edu/collabra/article/5/1/18/113004/Near-and-Far-Transfer-in-Cognitive-Training-A)).

## What we decided

The people who give tasks work with the child. They wait for a first try, read the error from what happened, and answer with one move: show the act, mark what matters, make the task smaller, or show it on another example. Learning to learn grows inside the tasks: little by little, the child checks the work before the person does. The world stays the judge of what is true, because young children cannot yet judge their own work well.

We changed our first rule because of the evidence. That is how we want to work.

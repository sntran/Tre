# The making of Tre

*[Tiếng Việt](README.vi.md)*

This is the diary of the making of Tre. Each entry tells one thing that we found while we made the game: a question, what the research or the history says, and what we decided. Each entry gives its sources, so that you can check them.

We write it for parents and for anyone who wants to know why the game is the way it is. Some entries are about how children learn; some are about the history and the customs of Vietnam.

## How an entry is made

- One file for each entry, in English (`YYYY-MM-DD-name.md`) and in Vietnamese (`YYYY-MM-DD-name.vi.md`), with the same content.
- Each entry has three parts: the question, what we found (with a link to each source), and what we decided.
- An entry tells only what the sources say. When nobody knows, the entry says so. When we chose with no evidence either way, the entry says that too.
- The rules that come from an entry go into `docs/DESIGN.md`; the details of the research go into `docs/research/`.
- Do not rewrite an old entry when a later finding changes a decision. Write a new entry, and link to the old one. The diary keeps what we knew at each time.
- The date of an entry is the date of the decision. The entries from 28 September to 4 October 2026 were written on 5 October 2026, from the design document, the history of the repository, and the issues.

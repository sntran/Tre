# Where is Núi Trâu?

*3 October 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-10-03-where-is-nui-trau.vi.md)*

In the legend, the enemy reaches the foot of Núi Trâu (Trâu Sơn), and Gióng drives them back. Tre puts its places at their real places, so we needed to know where Núi Trâu is. The answer was less sure than we thought, and our first place for it was probably wrong.

## What we found

- **The old geography book** of the Nguyễn, the *Đại Nam nhất thống chí*, says that Trâu Sơn, also called Vũ Ninh mountain, is east of the district of Quế Dương, 12 dặm, with hills one after another in a long line.
- **A temple keeps the story.** At the village of Châu Cầu (the old commune of Châu Phong, Quế Võ, Bắc Ninh), the old temple of Cô Tiên keeps the story of the king of Ân who died at the mountain ([Những điều mới biết về Ma Cô Tiên ở Châu Sơn](https://huvi.wordpress.com/2019/03/08/nhung-dieu-moi-biet-ve-ma-co-tien-o-chau-son/)).
- **Our first hill was probably another hill.** It was very probably Núi Dạm (Đại Lãm), the hill of Chùa Dạm in Nam Sơn. We found no source that calls it Trâu Sơn.
- **The real land matches the books.** The height data shows a line of low hills near Châu Cầu, about 4 km long, with tops of 50 to 70 m.
- **The exact hilltop is not known.** No source says which top it is.

Then we asked how to show a doubt to a child. Research says that a story teaches its errors, even when a general warning comes first; only a warning about that one detail stops it ([Butler et al. 2009](http://psychnet.wustl.edu/memory/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Butler-et-al-2009_PsychSci.pdf)).

## What we decided

- **Trâu Sơn is now the line of low hills near Châu Cầu.**
- **The game says what nobody knows, at the place:** "Today, people put Trâu Sơn in the low hills near the village of Châu Cầu, in Quế Võ. Nobody knows which hill it was."
- **On the way, the child finds the hill by real clues** from the old book: toward the sunrise, and "not one tall hill, but low hills one after another."

This became a rule: when nobody knows, the game says so, and it never makes up a doubt or a certainty that no source has.

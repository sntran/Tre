# A help that sees the work

*18 October 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-10-18-a-help-that-sees-the-work.vi.md)*

In the last play test, four testers who did not know the game played as children of grades K, 1, 2, and 4. At the mat of the teacher, a girl of nine put ten rods and asked for help. The teacher said "Lấy một que ở đống kia, rồi đặt lên chiếu nhé." So she thought that she had too few, put one more, and made it worse. At the river, after the fish swam out of the trap, the fisher said "Đủ hàng rồi. Xem nước lên nào!" A girl of six asked: "So is it good or bad??" Each line was right for another moment of the work, but not for this one.

## What we found

- **A help works when it answers the step that the child is at.** In a review of many studies, tutoring systems that respond to each step of the student's work were almost as effective as human tutors (an effect size of 0.76 against 0.79), and more effective than systems that only check the final answer (VanLehn 2011).
- **A good helper marks what the child just did.** In the classic study of a tutor with children of three to five, the help that worked marked the features that matter in what the child had just built, and kept the child on the goal (Wood, Bruner, and Ross 1976). A line that was written for the start of a task cannot mark what the child did at the end.
- **A false line costs trust.** The oldest tester said: "I didn't like it when grown-ups said things that weren't true." When a person in the village says a thing that the child can see is not so, the child learns to stop listening.

## What we decided

- **A help is the next step of the work as it is now.** Before a person speaks, the game reads the state of the work: a full mat gets the count and the tie, a row with a hole gets the hole, and a task that is done gets no hint. A test will bring each task to each state and check the line.
- **A tap always walks.** In the same test, the game moved the hero to a side of the mat by itself, into a corner where no tap could walk out. Two of the youngest children were stuck until they found the stick by chance. The game will never move the hero to such a place, and from any place the hero can walk out the way that it came.
- **The wave is everywhere.** In the raid of the river and at the hopscotch, the wave was hidden, and the children had no way to ask. The child will be able to call for help in every part of the game.

## The limit of what we know

The review is about older students and tutoring systems, not about a game with a world. We think that the same rule holds for a child of five, because a help that says a done step is wrong at any age, but we will check it: in the next play test we will count how often a help leads a child to the right next action.

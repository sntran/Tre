# A test from its own time

*24 October 2026 · [Tiếng Việt](2026-10-24-a-test-from-its-own-time.vi.md)*

After the Five Trials, the elder of Phù Đổng used to send the child to Văn Miếu in Thăng Long, for a test. A girl of grade 4 who had been to Hà Nội was glad: "Ooh Văn Miếu! My mom took me there, with the turtle stones." Then she looked at the map, which said "Thời Hùng Vương", the time of the Hùng Kings: "But wait... I thought Văn Miếu was built much later by vua Lý? And Thăng Long too." A tester in an earlier round said the same. They were right, and this entry tells what we changed.

## What we found

- **Văn Miếu came much later than the legend.** Văn Miếu was built in 1070 under Lý Thánh Tông. His son Lý Nhân Tông held the first exam of the country in 1075, and opened Quốc Tử Giám, the first school of higher learning, in 1076. The stone steles with the names of the doctors began in 1484, under Lê Thánh Tông. The city had the name Thăng Long from 1010.
- **The story of Thánh Gióng is set in the time of the sixth Hùng King,** a legend with no date. The archaeology of that land in the last centuries before our era is the Đông Sơn culture.
- **We already keep other things in their time.** Era 1 has no coins, because the first coins of Vietnam came with the Đinh (968–981), and no maize under the eaves, because maize came many centuries later. A temple of 1070 in the time of the Hùng Kings is the same kind of mistake, and a child who knows Văn Miếu learns a wrong picture from it.

## The test of the legend

The legend has its own test. It opens with the king's call: "Vua Hùng bèn sai người đi khắp nước rao cầu hiền tài giết giặc", the Hùng King sent people all over the country to look for people of talent to fight the invaders. When the messenger comes to the village, the boy Gióng speaks for the first time. In another legend of the same sixth Hùng King, the story of bánh chưng, the king tests his sons to choose who will rule after him. A test by the king belongs to that time.

## What we decided

- **In Era 1, the messenger of the Hùng King gives the tests.** After the Five Trials, he comes over the finished bridge into Phù Đổng and tests the child at the village hall (the placement test, for grade 3 and up). After the victory, he comes back with the thanks of the king and gives the exam of the era.
- **The title of Era 1 is a word of its time:** "người tài", a person of talent, from the messenger's call. It goes into the notebook as a print. The scholar titles (Tú tài and the others) and the stone stele come with the exams of Văn Miếu.
- **Văn Miếu comes in its own chapter,** the chapter of the Lý. The child sees it open and sits its first exam there. The girl who knows the turtle stones will see them in their time.
- **"Sắt" or "thép".** The smith says that hot iron gets hard in cold water. This is true of steel, which is iron with a little carbon; nearly pure iron does not get much harder. The things of the legend keep their words (ngựa sắt, roi sắt), but the lines that say what gets hard in cold water now say "thép", with a short line the first time: "Thép là sắt nung lâu trong lửa than." A person speaks the words of the village, but a line that teaches the science of a task must say it truly.

## The limit of what we know

Nobody knows when the Hùng Kings lived, or how the people of their time chose people of talent; the messenger's test is a part of the legend, not of history, and the game marks it as a legend. The legend also puts an iron horse in the time of the Ân, the Shang of China, which ended long before iron came to this land. We keep that, because it is the legend, and the game says so.

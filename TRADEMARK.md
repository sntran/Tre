## Forks

A fork must use a different name and a different logo. Before you publish a fork, do these steps:

1. Change the name in `i18n/vi.json`, `i18n/en.json`, `index.html`, and `manifest.webmanifest`.
2. Replace `art/logo.svg` and all icons.
3. Keep the license notices and the credit to the original project.

To ask for permission for other uses, open an issue in this repository.
